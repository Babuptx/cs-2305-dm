    Working reference

    Use your instructor’s permitted materials during actual exams.

    Logic
    p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q. Contrapositive: ¬q → ¬p. ¬∀x P(x) ≡ ∃x ¬P(x). Negation reverses quantifiers.
    Proof
    Direct: assume hypotheses, derive conclusion. Contradiction: assume the negated claim. Induction: establish base cases; assume earlier cases; prove the next case.
    Sets & functions
    |A ∪ B| = |A| + |B| − |A ∩ B|. A → B is injective if distinct inputs have distinct outputs; surjective if every codomain element is attained.
    Counting
    P(n,r) = n!/(n−r)!; C(n,r) = n!/[r!(n−r)!]. Independent choices multiply; disjoint alternatives add. Nonnegative solutions of x₁+⋯+xₖ=n: C(n+k−1,k−1).
    Graphs & arithmetic
    For finite undirected graphs: Σ deg(v) = 2|E|. A tree has |E| = |V|−1. a ≡ b (mod m) iff m divides a−b. Cancellation modulo m requires an invertible factor.
    Recurrences
    aₙ = r aₙ₋₁ gives aₙ = a₀ rⁿ. aₙ = aₙ₋₁+d gives aₙ = a₀+nd. Initial conditions select a unique solution. Induction and recurrence solving may vary by course section.
